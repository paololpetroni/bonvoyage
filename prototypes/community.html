How your score is built

  1. Community baseline. Everyone's overall stars, blended with the criterion scores weighted by your sliders. Places with few ratings are pulled toward the category average, so seven perfect reviews can't beat four hundred strong ones.
  2. Taste match. Raters tag what they experienced (spicy, sour, lively, historic). Tags add up to a profile for each place, matched against yours. Places nobody has rated yet use their type, the way a real import from a map database would know a restaurant is Thai.
  3. Travelers like you. Once you've rated places others have also rated, the app finds people whose ratings rise and fall with yours and predicts what you'd give a place from what they gave it. This carries more weight as your rating count grows, up to 75% at 15 ratings.
  4. Learning. With five or more detailed ratings in a category, the app checks which criteria move your overall score and tunes your weights. Your high and low ratings also nudge your flavor profile.

Your ratings stay in this browser only. In the real app they'd live in your account.
